### 100% BUSINESS OBJECT COVERAGE — MANDATORY

Business Object discovery and Business Activity discovery are tightly connected.

First identify ALL important real-world Business Objects involved in the business based on:
- BUSINESS IDEA
- BUSINESS DOMAIN
- PRIMARY REFERENCE BUSINESS
- INTERNET SEARCH RESULTS

Do not stop after identifying only the obvious objects.

For every Business Object identified, determine its complete lifecycle across the business.

For EACH Business Object, internally answer:

1. Where does this object originate, enter, or get created?
2. Which business activity first handles it?
3. How is it processed, managed, changed, selected, approved, fulfilled, delivered, used, cancelled, returned, resolved, completed, or otherwise handled?
4. Which subsequent activities handle it?
5. What happens to it at the end of its relevant lifecycle?
6. What business activities depend on this object?
7. Are there important exceptions or alternative paths affecting this object?
8. Is there any major business activity missing because this object was overlooked?

Every important Business Object MUST be represented by one or more Business Activities.

Every Business Activity MUST explicitly identify the Business Objects it handles.

### OBJECT COVERAGE RULE

Before producing the final JSON, internally construct an object-to-activity coverage map:

Business Object
→ Activity where introduced/created/received
→ Activity where processed/managed
→ Activity where fulfilled/moved/completed
→ Activity where cancelled/returned/resolved, if applicable
→ Final lifecycle activity, if applicable

Then validate:

- EVERY important Business Object appears in at least one activity.
- EVERY important Business Object is actually handled in the steps of those activities.
- EVERY important Business Object has its relevant lifecycle represented.
- EVERY activity references at least one Business Object.
- NO Business Object exists only as a name without being handled by an activity.
- NO important activity is omitted because an object was overlooked.
- NO activity exists only to artificially satisfy object coverage.
- Do not create duplicate or overlapping Business Objects.
- Do not create generic industry objects without evidence.
- Do not create technical/software/database objects.
- Do not invent lifecycle stages that are unsupported by the evidence.

### 100% COVERAGE GATE

The model MUST NOT finalize the response until the following is true:

100% of identified Business Objects
→ are mapped to one or more Business Activities
→ are explicitly handled within Activity Steps
→ have their relevant lifecycle represented
→ and are connected to the overall end-to-end business process.

If an important Business Object is discovered while deriving Activities, ADD it to businessObjects and update the Activities accordingly.

If an Activity reveals an important Business Object that was not previously identified, ADD that Business Object first, then ensure the Activity references it.

Business Object discovery and Activity discovery must therefore be performed together as a closed-loop process.

Do NOT output the coverage map or validation process.
Only output the final JSON.
